Kamgar Connect ("we", "us") runs kamgar.de, the Kamgar Connect mobile app and our phone registration line. We connect daily wage workers with people who want to hire them. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Workers: name, mobile number, city, skill, years of experience, expected daily wage, days or hours you are available, and (only if you choose to give it) your Aadhaar number.
  • Employers: name, mobile number, city, company name (optional), and the type and number of workers you need.
  • Location: if you allow it, your device's approximate GPS location, used to show nearby workers or jobs.
  • Phone registration calls: when you call our number, the answers you speak are converted to text and stored, together with your caller number. We do not keep audio recordings of these calls.
  • Job and call records: jobs posted and accepted, hire requests, call status (for example answered or missed), and ratings and reviews.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and browser type, kept for security and to fix problems.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you log in.
  • To show worker profiles (name, skill, city, experience, wage, availability and ratings) to employers looking to hire.
  • To call or message workers about job offers and to tell employers whether a worker accepted.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent misuse and fix problems.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you register or call our number. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 6).

3. Who can see it

  • Employers see worker profiles as described above. Your Aadhaar number is never shown to employers or other users.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us: Render (servers), MongoDB Atlas (database, hosted in Mumbai, India), Hostinger (website), Twilio (phone calls and SMS) and Google Gemini (in-app assistant chat). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal data within [30] days, except records we must keep by law.

5. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access to it is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to act for you. Contact us using the details below. We will respond within [30] days.

7. Children

Kamgar Connect is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
